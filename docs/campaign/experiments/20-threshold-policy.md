# EXP-20 Decision policy selection (N-of-M, confidence weighting, anchor gate)

Pre-registered BEFORE results. Workstream WT-20 (DecisionCalibration), slice S-05.

- Hypothesis: a confirmation policy with confidence-weighted N-of-M (`confirm_weight_sum`, `confirm_weight_gain`) and an anchor gate (`cascade_gate_threshold`) can reach **0 confirmed false positives on independently labeled negatives** while keeping **>=90% true-positive rate on positives**, where the shipped 2-of-3 rule cannot; hard negatives (WT-06 R5) that hover near `confirm_threshold` without a high-score peak are rejected by the weight sum and/or the anchor requirement without delaying true positives beyond one extra window.
- Requirement link: G-02 (0 confirmed FP on negatives), G-03 (>=90% TP), G-05 (all candidate values live in `config/thresholds.toml` only), F-14 (N-of-M + cooldown), F-15 (severity/fusion untouched here), WT-06 research R5 (hard-negative decision tuning).
