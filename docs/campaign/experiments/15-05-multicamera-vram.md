# EXP-15.05 Multi-camera allocation rule + VRAM inputs (1 and 2 workers)

The allocation rule `frame_pipeline.plan_inference_allocation` encodes this deterministically and never fabricates unmeasured VRAM inputs.
- Requirement link: WT-15 step 5; R-01 (pooling trigger at ≥3 cameras); F-06 (per-camera inference process); 12 GB ceiling.

- Candidate: the rule as implemented: N∈{1,2} → `dedicated-per-camera` (N processes, violence pool size N); N≥3 → `pooled-violence` (N camera processes + 1 shared violence pool process, pool size 1).
- Success criteria (defined BEFORE inspecting results): (a) simulation tests green (trigger at exactly 3, process counts, never-guess-unmeasured behavior); (b) measured VRAM at 1 and 2 workers recorded with provenance and fed into the rule; (c) 2-worker total GPU used ≤ 10.0 GB (≥2 GB headroom) verdict recorded (WT-16's criterion, reused for comparability); (d) the ≥3-camera pooling behavior is explicitly labeled UNMEASURED-GATE (no camera devices exist; 3 full trios on 16 GB RAM not attempted).
